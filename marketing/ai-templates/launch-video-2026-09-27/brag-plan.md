# Brag Plan: runsIT AI Templates

## Planning rubric

1. **App:** A beginner-friendly template store and private dashboard that turns an app idea into an AI plan, complete HTML guide, sample prototype, and full coding AI prompt.
2. **Strongest claim:** “Your idea. A head start.” Six starting points, with a complete guide and full prompt for a separate coding AI.
3. **Visual hook:** Warm paper, ink outlines, orange selection, yellow add-on cards, and the Plan / Build files / Add-ons dashboard.
4. **Actual UI to show:** The six-template store grid and the paid dashboard with plan, AI chat, guide, and prompt actions.
5. **Shortest satisfying video:** 24.8 seconds for the full flow plus the three optional extras.
6. **Tone:** `app-store`; friendly, confident product walkthrough for people who have ideas but no coding background.
7. **Audio:** Upbeat clean music with restrained UI clicks and two soft transitions.
8. **Share caption:** Got an app idea but no coding experience? Pick an AI template, shape your plan, then take the complete guide and full prompt into your coding AI.
9. **User flow:** Pick a template → describe/refine an idea in the dashboard → download a guide and copy a full prompt to a coding AI. Add-ons are optional.

## Angle

Make the intimidating leap from “I have an idea” to a practical first build feel tangible. Show the real product journey rather than promising that one paste magically finishes an app. The phrase “one full prompt” means one copyable starting prompt with implementation guidance; the coding AI still builds and tests the project.

## Hook

“Got an app idea? No coding experience?” appears over a warm, oversized brief card. The second line lands by 1.2 seconds and holds. That sets up the viewer, not the technology.

## Key moments

- The six real template names appear in a source-based storefront reconstruction; a cursor selects Mobile app.
- A labeled example project, “PlantLoop,” appears in the source-based dashboard. The plan's feature cards and AI chat show how the buyer shapes an idea; “20 edits included” is shown with the paid workspace.
- The complete HTML guide reveals setup, features, tests, launch, and a clearly labeled sample prototype. A full prompt is copied for a separate coding AI.
- The three real optional extras appear by name: App icon, AI teamwork, Skills & tools setup.

## Outro

“Your idea. A head start.” with “AI Templates by runsIT” and `runsit.ca/templates/`. A small “Optional launch help for supported projects” line covers the separately quoted service without implying it is bundled.

## Tone

- Preset: `app-store`
- Creative direction: warm, accessible product film with the website's bold outlines and direct language.
- Interpretation: quick interface motions, long enough holds for every label, no abstract AI imagery.

## Format and duration

- Landscape, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps.
- 24.8 seconds. No narration (the invocation did not request voice).

## Visual identity

- Background: `#ede4d3`
- Surface: `#fffdf7`
- Text: `#1b1a17`
- Accent: `#ff5a36`; selected-extra yellow: `#ffd23f`
- Display and body font: Figtree, from the project's local font assets.
- Strongest visual: the storefront's bordered six-card choice grid handing into the dashboard's plan and chat columns.

## Audio direction

- Role: clean upbeat bed, with four or five quiet interaction cues.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` from the brag bundle at roughly 0.34, eased in and out.
- Music cue guidance: bundled 120.19 BPM preset. Use 17.02 seconds for the full-prompt reveal and 21.01 seconds for the outro if readability allows. Six-card sequence can reference beats near 3.02, 4.02, and 5.03 seconds; all text remains settled after reveal. Add-on labels can reference 17.52, 18.52, and 19.52 seconds, then hold together.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle warmth/depth in a non-text paper glow, using extracted music data if supported.
- SFX posture: restrained; selection click, plan update click, guide reveal, copy action, final soft impact.
- Restraint rule: no keypress on every character, no loud impact on every card, no narration.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The idea — 2.8s (0.0–2.8)
On screen: “Got an app idea?” then “No coding experience?” above a simple idea card. The second line holds over one second. The brand paper and orange marker are immediately recognizable.
Sequential/interaction: two text beats, then a brief card opens.
Audio intent: music begins; one soft card sound.
Transition: clean push to the store.

### Scene 2 — Pick a template — 3.8s (2.8–6.6)
On screen: “Pick a starting point.” Show six real choices. A cursor selects Mobile app and the selection becomes orange. Storefront composition and names follow the source.
Sequential/interaction: cards arrive in groups, then a visible click. Each card stays visible until the cut.
Audio intent: a single UI click on selection.
Transition: the selected card expands into the workspace.

### Scene 3 — Shape the plan — 4.8s (6.6–11.4)
On screen: the dashboard's Plan, Build files, and Add-ons tabs; the Plan/Chat layout; example “PlantLoop” with “List a plant,” “Find nearby swaps,” and “Arrange a pickup.” A chat request “Make sign-up optional” produces a visible “Plan updated” status. Display “20 edits included.” Keep “Example project” visible.
Sequential/interaction: plan cards appear in a short cascade, followed by the chat edit and update.
Audio intent: quiet click on the sent chat request.
Transition: the plan turns into a guide page.

### Scene 4 — The full guide — 5.3s (11.4–16.7)
On screen: “A complete guide, in one HTML file.” A guide browser shows “Setup,” “Features,” “Tests,” and “Launch” with a “Sample prototype” phone panel and the real “Download complete HTML guide” button.
Sequential/interaction: guide navigation draws in; prototype becomes visible. The complete guide headline holds over two seconds.
Audio intent: soft reveal.
Transition: guide slides aside to expose build files.

### Scene 5 — One prompt, optional extras — 4.3s (16.7–21.0)
On screen: “One full prompt to start building.” Show the real “Copy full prompt” action leading to “Paste into your coding AI.” Alongside, reveal the three optional extras by name: App icon, AI teamwork, Skills & tools setup. All three settle and hold together.
Sequential/interaction: copy click; add-on cards arrive one by one.
Audio intent: click on copy, light accents for first and last add-on only.
Transition: warm cut to brand close.

### Scene 6 — Brand close — 3.8s (21.0–24.8)
On screen: “Your idea. A head start.” / “AI Templates by runsIT” / `runsit.ca/templates/`. Small footer: “Optional launch help for supported projects.”
Sequential/interaction: headline lands, CTA holds still for the final two seconds.
Audio intent: final soft impact, music fades.

**Music mood:** upbeat, approachable.
**Audio summary:** a clean bed supports visible clicks and reveals; the final brand frame gets room to breathe.
