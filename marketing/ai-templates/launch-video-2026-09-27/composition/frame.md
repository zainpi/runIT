# Frame direction

Warm paper and sturdy ink borders come from the actual AI Templates storefront and dashboard. The video enlarges the product interface for reading at 1920 × 1080. Orange means selection/action; yellow marks optional extras. Interface motifs should remain familiar: six template cards, Plan / Build files / Add-ons tabs, plan-and-chat columns, and the guide/prompt actions.

Treat “PlantLoop” and all plant-swap feature content as a visibly labeled example, never as a customer outcome. The sample prototype remains labeled as such. No generic AI glows, abstract circuits, or code rain.
