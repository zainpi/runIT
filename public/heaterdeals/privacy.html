HeaterDeals Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026
Provider: runsIT (Canada)
Contact: info@runs-it.com

HeaterDeals is a local-first iPhone app for reviewing price drops, saving deals, setting alert preferences, and opening Amazon links.

Data used by the current build

The app stores an app-specific Sign in with Apple identifier, onboarding choices, marketplace and alert preferences, saved deal IDs, and local subscription state on your device. Apple handles Sign in with Apple and App Store payment processing. HeaterDeals does not receive full payment details.

When you choose Buy on Amazon, the app opens Amazon's app or website. Amazon's privacy policy and terms apply to that destination. HeaterDeals may use an affiliate tag; your Amazon price does not change.

What we do not use

The current build does not request camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location, advertising identifiers, or behavioral analytics. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app tracking.

Notifications and deletion

Notifications are optional. The current build schedules a local trial reminder. Delete local account data from You → Account → Delete account. Apple retains Apple Account and App Store records under Apple's policies.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app's data practices change. Contact info@runs-it.com for privacy questions.
