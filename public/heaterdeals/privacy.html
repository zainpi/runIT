HeaterDeals Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026
Provider: runsIT (Canada)
Contact: info@runs-it.com

HeaterDeals helps you review price drops, filter deal signals, save deals on your device, configure alert preferences, and open an Amazon product or cart link. An active App Store subscription is required for the live deal feed and server-backed alert tools.

Information we use

When you use Sign in with Apple, we receive Apple's app-scoped user identifier and, if Apple provides it, an email or private relay address. We create a random app-account token that links your Apple account, StoreKit purchase, and HeaterDeals session. The app stores that token and short-lived session credentials in the iOS Keychain.

To verify access, the service processes App Store transaction and subscription identifiers, product ID, environment, status, expiration, revocation, and related verification data. Apple processes payment details; HeaterDeals does not receive or store your full payment card or bank information.

The service receives marketplace and alert rules you send to the API. The app currently keeps saved deal IDs and most presentation preferences on the device. The API uses an IP address and request timestamps for rate limiting, abuse prevention, duplicate-request controls, and security.

Deal and Amazon data

The server imports product and price signals from Keepa and stores normalized deal data such as ASIN, title, marketplace, price history, seller, Prime/FBA status, and heat-score inputs. Keepa credentials stay on the server and are never included in the app. When you choose Buy on Amazon, the app opens Amazon's app or website. Amazon's privacy policy applies there. HeaterDeals may use an affiliate tag; your Amazon price does not change.

What we do not use

HeaterDeals does not request camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or precise location. We do not use an advertising identifier, sell personal information, or use behavioral analytics or cross-app tracking.

Notifications, security, and retention

Notifications are optional. The app schedules a local trial reminder. Alert rules may be stored on the service so they can be protected by subscription and request controls. We use Apple, Supabase, Keepa, Cloudflare, and Amazon as described above. API requests are authenticated and rate limited; refresh tokens are stored as hashes on the service.

Use You → Account → Delete account to delete the HeaterDeals server account and associated alert, entitlement, session, and operational records, as well as local account state. Apple may retain Apple Account and App Store records under Apple's policies. Deleting a HeaterDeals account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Children, changes, and contact

HeaterDeals is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. We may update this page when our data practices change. Contact info@runs-it.com for privacy or deletion requests.
