Service

HeaterDeals provides a subscription-gated deal feed, price context, heat scores, saved-deal tools, alert preferences, and links to Amazon. Deal data is imported and normalized from third-party sources, including Keepa. Prices, inventory, seller availability, shipping, and checkout are controlled by Amazon or the applicable seller. Verify the final price and terms on Amazon before buying.

Subscription

HeaterDeals Monthly is an auto-renewable monthly subscription, product ID com.heaterdeals.subscription.monthly. The standard US price is $12.99/month. Eligible new subscribers may receive a 3-day free trial. After the trial, the subscription renews automatically unless canceled in Apple Account subscription settings before renewal. Apple handles billing, renewals, cancellations, and refunds.

The subscription provides access to the live deal feed and server-backed features while the App Store entitlement is active. We may apply rate limits and duplicate-request controls to protect the service.

Affiliate disclosure

Qualifying Amazon links may contain an affiliate identifier. Amazon may pay HeaterDeals a commission; your product price does not change. Amazon's terms and privacy policy apply to Amazon purchases.

Acceptable use and availability

Use HeaterDeals lawfully. Do not scrape or automate requests, bypass subscription or rate controls, interfere with the service, reverse engineer it, manipulate links, or violate Amazon's terms. The service is provided on an “as available” basis; a deal, alert, reference price, or Amazon cart link may change or disappear.

Deletion and contact

You may delete your HeaterDeals account from You → Account → Delete account. This deletes associated HeaterDeals records subject to lawful backup retention.
