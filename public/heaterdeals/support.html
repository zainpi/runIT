Cancel a trial or subscription

Open iPhone Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions → HeaterDeals and choose cancel before the next renewal. Apple manages billing and refunds.

Restore a subscription

Open HeaterDeals → You → Account → Restore purchases.

Delete account data

Open You → Account → Delete account and confirm. The current local-first build removes the profile identifier, preferences, saved deals, and local subscription mirror from the device.

Please include the app version and a description of the issue. Never send your Apple Account password or payment card details.
