Cancel a trial or subscription

Open iPhone Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions → HeaterDeals and choose cancel before the next renewal. Apple manages billing and refunds.

Restore a subscription

Open HeaterDeals → You → Account → Restore purchases. The app asks Apple to verify the current subscription and then links the verified transaction to your HeaterDeals account.

Delete account data

Open You → Account → Delete account and confirm. HeaterDeals deletes the server account and associated alert, session, entitlement, and operational records, then removes local account state. This does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel that separately in Apple Account settings.

The live feed will not refresh

Check your connection, confirm your Apple subscription is active, and try Heaters → refresh. Keepa and Amazon data can be delayed or unavailable briefly.

Include the app version, iOS version, and marketplace when contacting info@runs-it.com. Never send your Apple Account password, payment card details, or a Keepa key.
