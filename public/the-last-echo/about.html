About the studio

A small team building in public

The Last Echo is hand-built by a two-person independent team focused on readable systems, respectful progression, and responsive support.

Last updated July 21, 2026

What we are making

The Last Echo is a landscape idle auto-battle RPG in active development for mobile devices. The current playable build is available on the App Store. Google Play availability is planned for a future release.

Our work combines game design, engineering, pixel-art presentation, operations, and direct player support. Because the team is small, we keep public claims specific and distinguish current functionality from future plans.

Our development principles

  • Respect time. Routine progress should continue without requiring the game to remain open or demanding constant check-ins.
  • Preserve decisions. Automation should handle repetition, while equipment, ability, and upgrade choices remain understandable and meaningful.
  • Disclose probability. Randomised reward odds and hard-pity rules should be visible where the decision is made.
  • Keep free progression viable. Optional purchases must not become a requirement for completing core game content.
  • Document what exists. Guides describe the current game and include update dates so players can recognize when information may have changed.

How to contact us

For support, privacy questions, App Store issues, or corrections to this website, email info@runs-it.com. Helpful bug reports include the app version, device model, the screen where the issue occurred, the expected behavior, and what happened instead.
