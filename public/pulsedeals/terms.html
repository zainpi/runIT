Service

PulseDeals provides a subscription-gated deal feed, price context, heat scores, saved-deal tools, community deal signals, alert preferences, and links to Amazon. Deal data is imported and normalized from third-party sources, including Keepa. Prices, inventory, seller availability, shipping, and checkout are controlled by Amazon or the applicable seller. Verify the final price and terms on Amazon before buying.

Subscription

PulseDeals Standard offers auto-renewable weekly and yearly subscriptions, product IDs com.pulsedeals.subscription.weekly and com.pulsedeals.subscription.yearly. Standard US prices are $4.99/week or $89.99/year. Pro also offers weekly and yearly billing through com.pulsedeals.subscription.pro.weekly and com.pulsedeals.subscription.pro.yearly. The total localized charge and selected billing period are shown before purchase. Eligible new subscribers may receive a 7-day free trial. After the trial, the subscription renews automatically at the selected weekly or yearly price unless canceled in Apple Account subscription settings before renewal. Apple handles billing, renewals, cancellations, and refunds.

Standard includes all available deals in one selected country and linked private Discord access. Pro supports multiple countries without exclusive deals; Pro weekly costs US $12.99/week (localized by storefront), with an eligible 7-day trial across all five domains. Existing monthly subscribers retain their existing access and billing terms. A linked, verified paid Discord membership also unlocks the app without a separate app subscription. Access lasts while the underlying membership is active. Unlinking Discord removes Discord-derived app access and does not cancel either paid membership.

The subscription provides access to the live deal feed and server-backed features while the App Store entitlement is active. It may also provide access to the optional PulseDeals Discord server through a linked Discord account. Discord membership screening, server rules, and Discord's terms also apply. We may apply rate limits and duplicate-request controls to protect the service.

Referral weeks

When the referral program is available, each qualifying referral earns the inviter one free week. The invited person must create a new PulseDeals account after the program launches, add the inviter’s code before their first subscription, and start an Apple-verified introductory free trial. One referral code per new account; self-referrals, restores, renewals, and previous subscribers do not qualify. Paid conversion is not required. If Apple revokes the trial, its unspent reward is withdrawn.

Weeks accumulate and can be redeemed one at a time through Apple’s promotional offer on the inviter’s existing or previous Apple subscription. Apple confirms timing and the ongoing localized price. The subscription renews at that price after the free week unless canceled through Apple. A week stays reserved while Apple processes or schedules it; canceled or failed attempts may require a verification delay before retrying. Credits have no cash value, do not discount separately billed Discord memberships, and do not expire automatically while the account is open. Deleting the inviter’s account forfeits its unused credits.

Affiliate disclosure

Qualifying Amazon links may contain an affiliate identifier. Amazon may pay PulseDeals a commission; your product price does not change. Amazon's terms and privacy policy apply to Amazon purchases.

Acceptable use and availability

Use PulseDeals lawfully. Do not scrape or automate requests, bypass subscription or rate controls, interfere with the service, reverse engineer it, manipulate links, or violate Amazon's terms. The service is provided on an “as available” basis; a deal, alert, reference price, or Amazon cart link may change or disappear.

Deletion and contact

You may delete your PulseDeals account from You → Account → Delete account. This deletes associated PulseDeals records except limited referral transaction and reward records retained to prevent duplicate rewards, preserve another member’s earned credit, and resolve billing disputes, and subject to lawful backup retention and attempts to remove the PulseDeals Discord access role. Subscription cancellation is separate and must be completed in Apple Account settings. We may suspend access to protect the service, users, or third-party rights.
