Service

PulseDeals provides a free deal feed, price context, heat scores, saved-deal tools, community deal signals, alert preferences, and links to Amazon. Deal data is imported and normalized from third-party sources, including Keepa. Prices, inventory, seller availability, shipping, and checkout are controlled by Amazon or the applicable seller. Verify the final price and terms on Amazon before buying.

Ads and optional ad removal

All live deals, countries, saved items, votes and alerts are free. The free experience may show clearly labeled ads.

Monthly Ad Removal (com.pulsedeals.ads.monthly) is an optional auto-renewable monthly subscription that removes in-app ads while active. The localized price and period are shown before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Cancel in App Store Settings at least 24 hours before renewal. Cancellation preserves ad removal until the paid period expires. No trial is promised.

Lifetime Ad Removal (com.pulsedeals.ads.lifetime) is a one-time restorable non-consumable purchase removing in-app ads for the lifetime of PulseDeals, with no recurring charge. It does not guarantee the service operates forever. Restore with the same Apple Account. Buying lifetime does not cancel an existing monthly subscription; cancel monthly separately to avoid renewals.

Neither product unlocks deals or countries. Apple handles payments, cancellation, and refunds. Revoked/refunded purchases stop removing ads. Historical active Apple memberships remain eligible for ad removal under their existing billing terms. Discord access is separate. Referral rewards are paused; historical records and existing credits are retained, and no new reward is promised. Request and abuse controls still apply.

Amazon handoff

PulseDeals opens ordinary Amazon links without an affiliate identifier or referral commission. Amazon's terms and privacy policy apply to Amazon purchases.

Acceptable use and availability

Use PulseDeals lawfully. Do not scrape or automate requests, bypass purchase verification or rate controls, interfere with the service, reverse engineer it, manipulate links, or violate Amazon's terms. The service is provided on an “as available” basis; a deal, alert, reference price, or Amazon cart link may change or disappear.

Account access and contact

Use You → Account → Sign out to clear the local session. To request deletion of your PulseDeals account, contact info@runs-it.com. Deletion excludes limited referral transaction and reward records retained to prevent duplicate rewards, preserve another member’s earned credit, and resolve billing disputes, and is subject to lawful backup retention. Subscription cancellation is separate and must be completed in Apple Account settings. We may suspend access to protect the service, users, or third-party rights.
