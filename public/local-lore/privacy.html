Local Lore privacy

Effective September 10, 2026. Local Lore is a Toronto geography game operated by runsIT.

Your saved game

We use an essential, random browser cookie to recognize your player. We store its hashed identifier, your game choices, answers, map guesses, clues, scores, and discovered places in Cloudflare D1 for up to 90 days. There is no account, public leaderboard, or cross-device sync. Clearing the cookie disconnects this browser from its saved history; the stored history expires automatically.

Maps and abuse prevention

Google supplies Street View photos and map images through our server. Google receives requests for the places and map views shown. We do not request your device location. Google’s privacy policy and Maps terms apply to its services.

To limit automated abuse and image costs, we keep daily hashes derived from request IP addresses and request counters for up to two days. Our hosting provider Cloudflare also processes connection information and may keep operational logs under its own policies. We do not store Google image files in our database or create an image cache. We store panorama identifiers to locate the current photos.

Contact

Use the contact details on runsit.ca for privacy questions. Location names and coordinates come from OpenStreetMap contributors; our collection and sources are available under ODbL.
